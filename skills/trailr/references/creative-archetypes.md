# Creative archetypes

These films are ideas, not templates. Take the concept (a 3-act arc, a color per beat, a symbol as protagonist), not its concrete devices: don't copy elements such as cinematic letterbox bars or "Act 1" title cards unless the user asks for them. The structure can be felt without being named on screen.

Example copy is presented in English. Historical production notes retain the original format and voiceover language.

1. **Warm product walk-through (16:9, 44s)**: cream paper bg + one accent color, continuous camera along a hand-drawn line connecting scenes, glowing guide orb, two-tone headlines (second clause in accent), real UI cards with soft layered shadows, cold open on an emotional artifact (voicemail). (The original ends with a zoom-out over all scenes; we don't copy that ending.)
2. **Hype cut (16:9, 15s)**: hard cuts every 1.5–3s on the beat, 3D mascot rendered through a Bayer dither (three.js primitives + ordered-dither shader), grotesk + serif-italic accent words, keyword marker highlights, neo-brutalist cards (2–3px ink border, hard offset shadow), rubber-stamp moments ("Handled."), letters dropping into the wordmark.
3. **Color-per-beat (16:9, 38s)**: each beat owns a flat pastel background (peach → periwinkle → yellow → mint → sky → lavender → black), a persistent presenter avatar in a corner circle, question→answer copy ("And your money?" → "Paid out instantly."), floating brand coins, dithered animal mascot.
4. **Symbol film (16:9, 15s)**: almost no copy; the logo shape is the protagonist and morphs — terminal typing cold open (`deploy --prod`) whose text warps into particles, prism refracts light into the only burst of color, shape becomes a grid node over a four-word step sequence, dot-matrix world map with deploy arcs, particles collapse into the logo, wordmark. Monochrome, synth tones tied to events.
5. **Emotional 3-act film (16:9, 60s)**: Act 1 dark and letterboxed (cinematic bars), second-person serif copy ("You made something people love."), pain personified concretely (ID badges for roles you never applied for, jargon chips like W-8BEN / PCI DSS, "Chargebacks at 2 a.m." lockscreen, "stuck in a spreadsheet"), then music drops to silence on a single point of light ("breathe… let it"). Act 2 opens to full-frame white: one-line benefits with proof (a short claim + a globe counting up the countries served; a "handled." stamp; a sales notification on a lockscreen). Act 3 mission climax: particle ocean, counter to a big number, a one-line mission statement, then wordmark + a tagline that plays on the brand name.
6. **Illustrated sticker world (9:16, 42s)**: hand-drawn look — wobbly ink outlines (3–4px, pre-jittered paths), devices drawn as illustrations with thick colored outlines, flat color-block background per beat (cobalt, butter yellow, mint, coral, pink, teal) with organic blobs bleeding off the corners, polaroid sticker cards with handwritten captions ("courses", "launch day!"), script annotations with hand-drawn arrows (a usage number), falling coins, thank-you speech bubbles in several languages around a globe, torn-paper wipes. Cold open: a line draws itself into a laptop while "you make the thing." types. Serif display + grotesk + script accent. Ends on a dark card with a monogram tile + handwritten sign-off ("now go make the thing.").
7. **Night-to-day reveal (9:16, 39s)**: problem told at night (deep indigo, stars), solution in daylight (periwinkle sky, soft clouds). Hook "Your inbox is hiding money." on a phone inbox where one row glows and cash peeks out. The data arrives on a split-flap departures board (LHR→JFK · DELAYED 4H, CANCELLED boxed in red), which flips to a two-line message about money people are owed and never claim, with an UNCLAIMED stamp. "Meet [Brand]": a cute pod-robot mascot flies in; phone scans the inbox with a year counter rolling back (2026→2023) and a found-amount pill ticking up as boarding-pass cards pop. Climax: the total as a gold number on a sunburst. Then a one-click beat → "A few weeks later…" lockscreen payout notifications → the guarantee line → the CTA + URL pill. Accent: warm gold on the one key word.
8. **Founder-talk narration (16:9, 22s, 30fps)**: one casual run-on sentence spoken across beats instead of slogans — "Everyone wants to build a SaaS or an app… until it's time to do marketing. That's where most founders struggle. But now, you don't have to. Try this tool called [Brand]." Small left-aligned headline that keeps growing word by word; serif-italic accent words in the brand orange with self-drawing scribble underlines and doodle sparks. Mascot = the app icon itself with a face (orange tile with the logo glyph), peeking from a corner, then big and bouncing on the turn. Proof: a pile of real-looking X posts ("marketing is hard"); the turn cuts from warm grey to a full-bleed orange gradient; logo reveal with sparkles. Product flow shown literally: type a URL → click the CTA → analysis checklist ticks (Product, Audience, Brand voice, Positioning) → generated carousel cards fan out → word-swap platform list (Instagram, TikTok, X, Threads, Reddit) building a post grid → autopilot toggle + "On autopilot" circular stamp → scrolling wall of carousel themes → dark beat "So you can automate your marketing, with ease." with "posted" notifications → end card logo + mascot peeking + "Turn your SaaS into content that converts." + CTA pill + URL.
9. **Painted myth / brand allegory (16:9, 30s)**: the product told as a metaphor instead of a demo — adtech as alchemy. Painterly gouache/risograph artwork in a tight 3-color palette (cobalt blue sky, ochre mountains, cream clouds, orange fire accent); two characters: a young developer in a hoodie with a work lanyard and a wizard with a cauldron on a mountaintop. Almost no text until the end. Beats: she looks up at the wizard's peak → match cut into the wizard reflected in her glasses → fire-orange flash → three stages named like alchemy steps in small serif italics with a mono subtitle ("I. Separation · targeting" over an endless crowd in a valley; "II. Multiplication · creative" where one sneaker ad multiplies into a grid with a counter from 2 to 10,000 variants; "III. Distribution · delivery" with billboard screens on the mountain showing Meta / YouTube / Display) → both at the cauldron → a mountain of gold coins and her laughing in a coin shower → she now wears the wizard's hat → one serif line that names the metaphor (adtech turns data and compute into money) + small wordmark; the last shot loops back to the first. Artwork comes from an illustrator or image/video model; the code does the layered parallax, UI composites, counters, coin shower, type and sound.
10. **Debt-collection AI startup (built with this skill, 16:9 and 9:16, 52s)**: dark cold open with a pile of overdue-notice cards and a scribble, "Debt collection is bro-ken." cracking in two; lime iris into "Meet your new AI credit management team."; camera over a world canvas through agent cards switching on, a WhatsApp negotiation ending in a "Payment plan agreed" sticker, a formal notice signed and stamped "SENT VIA CERTIFIED EMAIL", a dashboard with the guide riding the chart line, a big neo-brutalist button click, then into the lime end card where the guide tile becomes the logo. (The first cut had a zoom-out over all the scenes before the end card; it was rejected, don't repeat it.) Brand: lime #C1FF4C, ink #141414, Hedvig Letters Serif + Inter + JetBrains Mono.
11. **Hotel concierge AI corporate film (built with this skill, 16:9 4K, 82s, Spanish male VO; approved, use as the reference for corporate B2B films)**: AI concierge for hotels on WhatsApp, made for hotel chain owners. Five acts. (1) Cold open on premium stock (aerial resort pool → chandelier lobby → gloved housekeeping → hotel at night) with white word-by-word lines "Guest service does not end at reception." / "Guests need help at any hour." and a WhatsApp notification sliding in over the night building; white wipe out. (2) Problem on white: three phones fill with repetitive guest questions, "Simple questions. Again and again." (3) Reveal: logo, then one phone + keyword stack "A virtual concierge on WhatsApp." → "Right in WhatsApp / 24/7 / Multilingual", with EN/FR phones answering. (4) How it works: guest asks for the wifi, the phone sits next to a flat hotel-info dashboard screenshot whose WiFi card highlights with a connector line ("Your hotel decides how [Brand] responds."), then the conversations view ("Your team sees everything in real time."). (5) Benefits as a growing left list beside a phone (fewer repetitive queries, more time for the team, guests served 24 hours, a more connected experience) → end card logo + "Your guest asks. [Brand] answers." Brand #004AAD + white, #86A7D4 only for details, Codec Pro (substituted with Plus Jakarta Sans), simulated UI only, no numbers on screen without client confirmation. Music: 120 BPM D major, intro bar, problem ticking, breath, impact on the logo, groove, final chord; mixed to -14 LUFS. Took 2 rounds: v1's opening had a cheap low-res facade and a bellboy shot; v2 replaced them with premium footage and 2 s more opening, and was approved.
12. **Home service ad, "rewind" (built with this skill, 9:16, 18s, 30fps, text + synthesized music only)**: batch-cooking-at-home service. Wednesday night, a nearly empty fridge, "Bread and cheese again."; the frame rewinds to Monday 10:00; the doorbell rings and the door opens on the cook carrying groceries, "Your cook arrives."; in the kitchen a wall clock runs 10→14 while containers fill, "Four hours of cooking in your home."; back to Wednesday, the fridge is full, "Lunch and dinner, Monday through Friday." (held ~3 s); end card logo + "A cook in your home" + URL. Cream, dark green and lime, Bricolage Grotesque + Inter. Took 3 rounds: v1 was only before/after (service not understood), v2 added the rewind and the cook, v3 moved 1.5 s from the end card to the full-fridge shot and recentered the chef hat.
