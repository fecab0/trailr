# Corporate video: stock footage and voiceover

For B2B corporate films (hospitality, real estate, services) where the viewer must feel a real place, mix live-action stock in the cold open with code-built UI for the rest.

For finding, licensing and downloading the clips, read [Stock video](stock-video.md).

- **Footage choice decides the price perception.** Target the buyer (e.g. the owner of a hotel chain): aerial resort/pool, chandelier lobby, gloved housekeeping close-ups, a night exterior with lit windows. Avoid flat daytime facades, visible brand signs, cliché staff poses (a smiling bellboy) and people in masks. Clips must be 4K, slow camera moves, 3–4 s each, crossfaded (~0.4 s) on the bar grid.
- **Montage**: cut the clips with ffmpeg into one `stock/montage.mp4` at the film's fps and resolution. Render the overlay scenes as transparent PNG frames (`page.screenshot({omitBackground:true})`, transparent body) and composite in ffmpeg: `[0:v]scale=W:H,trim=start_frame=a:end_frame=b,setpts=PTS-STARTPTS[bg];[bg][1:v]overlay=shortest=1`. The rest renders as JPEG as usual.
- **Text over footage**: white headline bottom-left, word by word, with a soft dark gradient behind it; a WhatsApp-style notification sliding in at the top right is a strong hook that bridges to the phone scenes.
- **Download access**: use the provider's offered download route as described in [Stock video](stock-video.md). If downloads are blocked, use another approved source or have the user download the chosen clip; do not work around access restrictions using unrelated origins or profiles.
- **Voiceover**: when requested, follow [Spoken narration](voiceover.md) for source selection, setup, script approval, generation, timing and music ducking. Use the official ElevenLabs MCP for ElevenLabs speech; supplied audio and an available local engine remain options. A corporate format does not automatically require speech.
- **Retiming**: change the scene's `bars` (or `sec`) in `script.json` and re-run `build-script.js`, `export.js` and `synth.py`; everything after it moves with it, including SFX, music and VO takes, which start at their scene start (see [Pacing and scene durations](pacing.md)). The stock montage is cut to the scene starts in `timeline.json`, so re-cut it too. Only in an old build without the scene table: wrap the page clock (`const core=render; window.render=t=>core(t-OFF)` — never `render(t-OFF)` on itself, that recurses), shift `SFX` cues by OFF, shift music and VO in `synth.py` by the same OFF, and add an intro bar for the new time.
- **Brand constraints from a client brief beat the defaults**: phones only (no laptops), dashboards as flat screenshots on white, no particles or decorative objects, no prices or CTA, a closing tagline instead of a URL pill.
